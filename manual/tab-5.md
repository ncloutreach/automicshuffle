Question 1:Which element is commonly used in the manufacturing of rechargeable batteries and also serves as a mood stabilizer?

A) Lithium (Li)

B) Cobalt (Co)

C) Nickel (Ni)

D) Zinc (Zn)

Answer: A) Lithium (Li)


Question 2:Which inert gas is commonly used in lighting and cryogenic refrigerants?

A) Helium (He)

B) Neon (Ne)

C) Argon (Ar)

D) Krypton (Kr)

Answer: B) Neon (Ne)


Question 3:Which transition metal is used in catalytic converters and also found in jewellery?

A) Palladium (Pd)

B) Platinum (Pt)

C) Rhodium (Rh)

D) Silver (Ag)

Answer: B) Platinum (Pt)


Question 4:Which element is used in water treatment and disinfectants and is known for its greenish-yellow gas form?

A) Fluorine (F)

B) Chlorine (Cl)

C) Bromine (Br)

D) Iodine (I)

Answer: B) Chlorine (Cl)

Question 5:Which actinide element is primarily used as nuclear fuel and also in depleted uranium weapons?

A) Thorium (Th)

B) Plutonium (Pu)

C) Uranium (U)

D) Americium (Am)

Answer: C) Uranium (U)
