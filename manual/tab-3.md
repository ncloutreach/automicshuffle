Students begin by selecting the matching game from the menu. A grid of cards is displayed, each containing either an element name, its chemical symbol, a property or a use. Player flip two cards at a time to attempt a match. For example, oxygen may be paired with its symbol “O” its property colourless and odourless or its use supports combustion. If the cards form a correct match, they remain paired on the grid. If the match is incorrect, the cards are flipped back. 

Correct matches earn points. Incorrect matches may carry a time penalty or point deduction. Students must race against a timer to make as many correct matches aspossible within the given time limit. This creates excitement and encourages faster recall of the facts. As they play, students are repeatedly exposed to key facts about elements, reinforcing their understanding of symbols, properties and uses in a memorable way.
